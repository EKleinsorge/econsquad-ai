The Monday AI for ED Drop — Issue #5: Your Community Profile Is Your AI Secret Weapon

June 9, 20264 min read

Every economic developer has a community profile packed with demographics, labor data, infrastructure specs, and quality-of-life stats that took months to compile. Most of the time, it gathers dust between formal updates. This week, let us change that.

This Week’s Tips

Tip 1

Turn Static Data Into Dynamic Talking Points

Your community profile is a goldmine that most EDs only tap at proposal time. Upload it to your AI workspace and prompt: “Using our community profile, create five compelling talking points for a logistics company considering a 150-job distribution center. Emphasize workforce within 60 miles, highway access, and utility capacity.” You will get crisp, tailored copy in seconds. Do this for your top three target sectors and by Friday you will have ready-made narratives for manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare sitting in a shared folder, ready to deploy when the next call comes in.

Tip 2

Spot Your Profile Gaps Before Prospects Do

AI is excellent at exposing what you do not have. After uploading your profile, ask: “What questions from a typical site selector RFI would our community profile NOT be able to answer?” The response will surface missing data points such as broadband speeds, childcare capacity, housing inventory, and permitting timelines. This is a pre-RFI audit, not a critique. You now have a prioritized list of data to collect before the next inquiry hits. Run this exercise quarterly and you will progressively close the gaps that send prospects to better-prepared competitors.

Tip 3

Generate Sector-Specific One-Pagers in Minutes

A logistics prospect wants different data than a biotech firm. Rather than maintaining separate documents for each sector, maintain one master profile and use AI to slice it on demand. Prompt: “From our community profile, extract data most relevant to a medical device manufacturer: workforce with STEM credentials, healthcare proximity, clean room facility availability, and university research partnerships.” Paste the result into a branded template and you have a sector-specific one-pager in under 20 minutes. You are always working from your most current data.
