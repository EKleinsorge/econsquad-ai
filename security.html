Security & Data Handling

Your work stays yours.

Written for the IT director, not the marketing brochure. What we send where, what we keep, who can see it, and what we don't yet have.

Last reviewed 1 September 2026

The short answer

No. Nothing you put into a specialist is used to train an AI model, and nothing you put in is reachable by another customer.

Those are two separate guarantees with two separate mechanisms behind them, and they are worth understanding separately, because most vendors answer only the first.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. This is the specific risk your IT provider is warning you about with free consumer AI tools, and it is a real one — consumer tiers of most AI products operate under materially different terms from their business APIs.

EconSquad AI does not run on consumer AI accounts. Your work is processed through commercial APIs whose terms prohibit training on customer data by default:

What it powersProviderTheir stated policy
The 22 specialists — every draft, grant narrative, RFI response and BRE survey OpenAI
API platform		 “By default, we do not use your business data for training our models.”
ARIA's performance and ROI analysis Anthropic
commercial API		 “By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products to train our models.”

Both providers retain API traffic briefly for abuse monitoring — OpenAI states up to 30 days, after which it is deleted — and neither feeds it back into model training. Zero-retention arrangements are available from OpenAI for qualifying use cases; if your organisation requires one, tell us and we will pursue it for your account rather than tell you it already exists.

If your team already uses a paid business tier of ChatGPT or Claude, your IT department has almost certainly already reviewed these same terms. EconSquad AI sits on the same commercial footing — not the consumer one.

Can another customer's query reach our information?

No, and the reason is worth being precise about.

Your work is stored in your own account in our database. Access is enforced by row-level security in PostgreSQL — a rule evaluated by the database itself on every single query, not a filter applied by our web application. A different customer's session is not permitted to read your rows at all. There is nothing for a query to reach.

This matters because the two are often confused. An application filter is code that decides what to show you; a bug in it leaks data. Row-level security is the database refusing to return rows that are not yours, regardless of what the application asks for.

Concretely:

  • There is no shared knowledge base. Your documents are not indexed into a pool that other customers' specialists draw from. Each specialist starts from its own instructions and the text of your request, and nothing else.
  • One conversation does not inform another — not another customer's, and not another user's inside your own organisation.
  • Google credentials are write-only from the browser. If you connect Gmail, the stored token cannot be read back by any signed-in user, including you. Only our server can use it, and only to serve your own mailbox back to you.

Who at EconSquad AI can see our work?

Administrative staff can access task history for support purposes. Today that is a very small team. We are telling you this plainly because a vendor who claims nobody can see your data is either mistaken or has no way to help you when something breaks.

If your organisation needs that access restricted, logged, or contractually limited for your account, raise it with us before you buy and we will put it in writing.

What does connecting Gmail actually grant?

Gmail and Calendar are optional and off until you turn them on. Connecting goes through Google's own consent screen, which lists exactly what is being granted, and you approve it yourself — we never ask for your Google password and could not accept it if offered.

You can revoke access at any time, either from inside EconSquad AI or from your Google Account permissions page directly, without our involvement. Your mail is read to display it to you and is not copied into our database.

What we don't have yet

Any vendor can write a reassuring security page. Here is what we would be overclaiming if we left it out:

  • No SOC 2 report. We are a new product and have not completed a third-party audit. If your procurement process requires one, tell us and we will give you an honest answer about timing rather than a vague one.
  • No signed DPA or BAA as standard. If your organisation requires a data processing agreement, we will negotiate one — just ask.
  • No HIPAA, CJIS or FedRAMP authorisation. EconSquad AI is not built for protected health information, criminal justice data, or federal systems requiring FedRAMP. Do not put that class of data into it.
  • Public records. If you are a public agency, your own retention and disclosure obligations still apply to work created here, exactly as they would in a word processor. We are a tool, not a records system.
